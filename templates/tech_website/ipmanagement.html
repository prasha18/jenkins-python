IP Management

IP Management

TNTTFC offers comprehensive support for the end-to-end management of intellectual property (IP) to help innovators secure and strategically leverage their creations. Our services are designed to align with both national and international IP frameworks, ensuring robust protection and long-term value generation.

  • IP Filing and Registration Support: We assist with the documentation, drafting, and filing of various forms of IP—including patents, trademarks, copyrights, and industrial designs. Our experts guide stakeholders through procedural requirements to ensure timely and effective registration.
  • Protection and Compliance Strategies: TNTTFC helps innovators develop strategies to safeguard their IP assets from infringement. We offer advisory on compliance with regulatory standards and maintain IP portfolios in accordance with evolving legal frameworks.
  • Monetization and Licensing Guidance: We support innovators in unlocking the commercial value of their IP through licensing, technology transfer agreements, and strategic partnerships. Our goal is to convert protected knowledge into revenue-generating opportunities.
