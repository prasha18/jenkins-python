Seed Grant

Seed Grant

TNTTFC supports early-stage innovators by facilitating access to seed funding for proof-of-concept validation, prototype development, and early market testing. We curate and disseminate information on a wide range of central and state funding schemes such as BIRAC, MeitY, DST, and EDI–IVP. In addition, we assist applicants with proposal writing, budgeting, and aligning their submissions with funding agency objectives. Our team also provides guidance on documentation and compliance to enhance approval rates and ensure smooth fund disbursement, empowering innovators to take their first steps toward commercialization with confidence.

Who Should Apply?

Individuals with innovative startup ideas
Teams with promising technical concepts
Early-stage startups looking to refine their approach
Researchers looking to commercialise their IP
