Technology Transfer

Technology Transferred

Technology transfer is a catalyst for translating research into meaningful societal and economic outcomes. Through the Tamil Nadu Technology Transfer Facilitation Centre (TNTTFC), we actively bridge the gap between academic innovation and industry application. By enabling successful technology transfers across institutions and sectors, we are fostering a culture of commercialization, collaboration, and value creation that drives Tamil Nadu’s innovation economy forward.
