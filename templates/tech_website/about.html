About TNTTFC

To ignite a culture of innovation-to-impact by transforming breakthrough ideas into market-ready solutions, empowering researchers, startups, and industries to co-create the future.   Tamil Nadu Technology Transfer Facilitation Centre (TNTTFC) aims to be the catalyst that bridges the gap between research and real-world application, strengthening IP ecosystems, fostering deep collaborations, and positioning Tamil Nadu as a global epicenter for Deep and Emerging Tech commercialization.

The Hon’ble Minister for Information Technology & Digital Services (IT&DS) Dr. Palanivel Thiaga Rajan on 17th July 2025 inaugurated the Tamil Nadu Technology Transfer Facilitation Centre (TNTTFC) for DeepTech Innovations Inaugurated at Tamil Nadu Technology (iTNT) Hub at the Anna University Campus.

Our Vision

To transform Tamil Nadu into a global leader in Deep and Emerging Tech innovation and commercialization by establishing a collaborative and scalable technology transfer ecosystem.

Our Mission

To nurture a thriving environment where Ideas evolve into global technological breakthroughs.

Supported by Tamil Nadu State Planning Commision

The Tamil Nadu State Planning Commission aids in formulating, implementing and evaluating policies related to development, economic growth, social welfare and innovation in Tamil Nadu. It engages in the review, evaluation, and monitoring of various welfare schemes and programmes of the Government, to identify bottlenecks and challenges in the last-mile connectivity of these programs and to ensure effective public service delivery to grassroots levels.

The Planning Commission’s broad focus areas include the preparation of plans, strategy, and goal-setting, along with monitoring, evaluation, and policy advocacy. Also, creating policy coherence and ensuring the effective implementation of our special programmes, which include State Balanced Growth Fund (SBGF), Tamil Nadu Innovations Initiative (TANII) and Tamil Nadu State Land Use Research Board (TNSLURB).

TNTTFC is supported & funded by the State Planning Commission under the Tamil Nadu Innovation Initiatives (TANII).

iTNT Hub’s JIGSAW Portal

JIGSAW is a pioneering digital innovation platform created by iTNT Hub to accelerate collaboration between academia, industry, startups, and investors across Tamil Nadu. Launched by the state’s leadership in early 2024, JIGSAW serves as a central gateway for synergistic partnerships that drive research, development, technology transfer, and solution commercialization in Deep and Emerging Technologies.
